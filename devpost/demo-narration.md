# Money Garden demonstration script

Target length: 2 minutes 40 seconds. Record the actual running Preview. Keep the budget sequence uncut and use the final verification report when describing tests.

## 0:00–0:10 — show the garden immediately

“Money Garden makes three abstract ideas visible: budgeting, progressive taxes, and decision logic. It's designed for anyone, with no finance or programming background needed.”

## 0:10–0:55 — practice and budget, continuous recording

Start growing. Drag the practice token to Essentials and begin the budget. Allocate 500 to Essentials, 300 to Reserve, and 200 to Choice. Run month.

“Move a token into a bowl, or use the large Allocate buttons. Every token represents 100 fictional credits. The remaining balance makes the tradeoff visible, and Undo lets you revise. This scenario asks for 500 in essentials and a reserve for a 200-credit surprise. When the surprise arrives, the reserve falls from 300 to 100. No debt or automatic borrowing is invented.”

## 0:55–1:30 — tax layers

Explore taxes. Predict Rise. Remove 500, add 500, then Compare.

“The tax example is separate from the monthly budget and uses practice rates. Each column splits income into retained money and tax. Going from 2,500 to 3,000 adds 100 tax, but leaves 400 more retained income. The higher rate applies to the next layer; it doesn't suddenly apply to everything.”

## 1:30–2:05 — logic

Try logic. Keep A true and B false with OR. Predict Open and Run. Switch to AND, predict Closed, and Run.

“This purchase policy requires both conditions: essentials covered and the reserve surviving the purchase. OR opens the gate when either is true. AND stays closed here because one check fails. The moving paths and gate reveal what the rule actually does. OR is useful for other policies; the operator must match the goal.”

## 2:05–2:25 — complete and repeat

Finish. Show the three takeaways, then New scenario.

“The experience ends with three takeaways and a fresh scenario. Learners can repeat, change their choices, and see different consequences. Details, mute, restart, and recenter remain available.”

## 2:25–2:40 — process and evidence

“I planned the experience with Devpost Learn and built it with CLAD and native Lens Studio tools. The source includes the design brief, lesson content, and acceptance tests. This demonstration uses Lens Studio Preview; device comfort and real hand tracking still need hardware testing.”

Before recording, replace the final process wording with the participant's own accurate account. Do not describe tests as passing until the final report records that result.
