# Cashfall demo narration (about 90 seconds)

**[Welcome card]** This is Cashfall. Money lessons usually live in spreadsheets. On Specs the lesson is a machine on your table. Pinch to place it.

**[The Falls]** Your paycheck lands at the top. Four thousand dollars falls down three tax steps. The first thousand pays nothing. The next two thousand pays ten percent. Only the dollars above three thousand pay twenty. Those are the coins arcing into the tube. Everything else lands in the basin: thirty-six hundred take-home.

**[Explore]** Change the paycheck and watch the tube. Here's the myth: a raise pushes you into the twenty percent step, so you lose money. Run it. Plus five hundred gross, plus four hundred in your pocket. Only the new dollars see the top rate.

**[The Split]** Take-home becomes ten coins. Pinch one, drop it in a jar. Needs first, then a cushion, then wants. Run the month. Rent gets paid, then a surprise bill hits the SAVE jar. The cushion held.

**[Growth]** Keep that habit for ten years at five percent and the column on the right is what you end up with.

**[The Gate]** A purchase shows up. Two checks light from your own split: needs covered, cushion intact. The AND gate only opens when both are true. Flip the checks, switch to OR, run the cart, and fill the truth table yourself.

**[Summary]** One month, three ideas, one machine on your table. Replay it, try a new scenario, or just play.
