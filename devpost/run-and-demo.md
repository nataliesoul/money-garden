# Run and demo guide

## Run

1. Open `Learn_AR_with_Specs.esproj` in Lens Studio 5.24.1 or a compatible 5.2x build and wait for assets to import.
2. Select the SPECS27 Interactive Preview. Mouse clicks stand in for pinches; click-drag moves the grabbable coin.
3. On the welcome card choose **Place in front of me** for the Preview (or **Place on a table** on hardware: aim at a flat surface, pinch when the reticle says "Pinch to place here").
4. Follow the console. The dock (top-right of the console) has sound, bring-it-here, restart and help.

For screenshots in the Preview, reset the preview camera and look at about (0, −22, −112); the machine then fits the frame with the console at the bottom.

## Three-minute demo path

| Time | Do | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Place in front of me | "Your paycheck is about to land on your table." |
| 0:15 | Show me the steps → Next ×3 | "Only the dollars on a step pay that step's rate. $4,000 becomes $400 of tax and $3,600 in the basin." |
| 0:50 | Let me try → + $500 twice | "Watch the tube: the top slice is the only thing that grows at 20%." |
| 1:10 | Myth check → No, it goes up | "A raise never lowers take-home. +$500 gross is +$400 for you." |
| 1:30 | On to the budget → Start splitting → tap NEEDS ×5, WANTS ×3, drag two coins to SAVE | "Every coin gets a job. Needs first, then a cushion, then wants." |
| 2:00 | Run the month | "Rent gets paid, then a $650 bill hits SAVE. The cushion held." |
| 2:15 | See it grow | "Keep that $720 a month and in ten years it is $111,800." |
| 2:30 | On to the gate → Try the AND gate → It is blocked | "Your own split lights the checks. AND needs both." |
| 2:45 | Fill the truth table → flip B, Run the cart | "Both true, gate opens, cart rolls through." |
| 2:55 | Skip ahead → AND → See your month | "One month, three ideas, on your table. Replay or try a new scenario." |
